### The stencil and the cloth

Katagami are paper stencils used to print patterns onto kimono fabric. Craftspeople cut each design by hand, laid the stencil on the cloth and brushed rice paste through it, so that the dye took everywhere except under the paste. **The stencil carries the design and the cloth carries the substance.**

A theme works the same way: it holds the layout, the type and the colour, while the words stay in plain text that editors can read and change without touching any markup.
